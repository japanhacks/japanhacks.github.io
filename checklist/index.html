First 30 Days in Japan Administrative Checklist

Step-by-step required municipal and legal procedures for foreign residents arriving in Japan.

Day 1 - 3

Receive Residence Card at Airport Immigration

~30 mins · Airport Immigration Counter

Present passport with landing permission visa at Narita, Haneda, Kansai, or Chubu airport to get physical Zairyu Card.

Day 1 - 3

Register Address at Ward Office / City Hall

~60 mins · Local Municipality / Ward Office

Submit Moving-In Notification (Tennyu-todoke) within 14 days of moving into permanent address.

Official Guidebook Info
Week 1

Enroll in National Health Insurance (NHI)

~30 mins · City Hall Health Insurance Division

Complete health insurance registration at City Hall if not covered by employee Shakai Hoken.

Official Guidebook Info
Week 1

Get Japanese Voice & Data Mobile SIM Card

~45 mins · Online or Electronics Retailer (Yodobashi/Bic Camera)

Contract a local Japanese phone plan (e.g. UQ Mobile, LINEMO, ahamo, Mobal). Required for bank registration.

Week 2 - 3

Open Japanese Bank Account (Japan Post Bank / Yucho)

~60 mins · Japan Post Bank / Online Bank

Open a local bank account for salary deposit, utility auto-debit, and rent payments.

Week 2 - 3

Apply for Plastic My Number Card

~15 mins · Online Portal or Photo Booth

Use the QR code on your notification slip to apply online or via smartphone photo booth.

Month 1

Verify National Pension Enrollment / Apply for Student Exemption

~30 mins · City Hall Pension Section

Ensure pension booklet arrives or apply for Gakusei Nofu Tokurei student exemption.
