How do I claim back the 20.42% pension tax refund after leaving Japan?

I recently left Tokyo and submitted my Lump-Sum Pension refund claim. I received ~80% of the money in my overseas bank account. How do I get back the remaining 20.42% income tax withheld by Japan Pension Service?

#pension#tax-refund#tax-representative

2 Community Answers

Kenji T.
ACCEPTED ANSWER

You must designate a Tax Representative (納税管理人 - Nōzei Kanri-nin) who resides in Japan BEFORE or after leaving. Once Japan Pension Service sends you the Notice of Lump-sum Withdrawal Payment (脱退一時金支給決定通知書), mail the ORIGINAL notice to your Tax Representative. They will file a Form 確定申告 at your former municipal tax office to get the 20.42% refunded into their Japanese bank account, which they can then transfer to you.

Make sure you keep the original blue paper notice in your mail! Tax offices in Japan will not accept photocopies for the 20.42% tax refund claim.
