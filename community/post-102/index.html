Do I need to pay 礼金 (Reikin / Key Money) when renting an apartment?

I am moving to Osaka next month. Some real estate agents quote 1 month Reikin and 1 month Shikikin. Is Reikin mandatory or negotiable?

#housing#reikin#renting-osaka

1 Community Answers

Takuya S.
ACCEPTED ANSWER

Reikin (Key money) is a non-refundable gift to the landlord. While common in Tokyo and Kansai, many modern UR Housing (UR賃貸住宅) or foreign-friendly agencies (like Village House or GaijinPot housing) offer Zero Reikin apartments. During off-peak seasons (May to November), you can often negotiate Reikin down to 0.
