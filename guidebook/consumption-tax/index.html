Consumption Tax (Shouhizei)

Summary: Japan’s 10% national consumption tax on most goods and services, with an 8% reduced rate that applies to groceries and food.
🎥Official MOJ Orientation Video #10: Taxes

Consumption Tax

Japan imposes a 10% consumption tax on goods and services, with an 8% reduced tax rate for groceries and food items.
