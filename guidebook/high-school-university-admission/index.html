High School, Vocational & University Admissions

Summary: High school entrance examinations, MEXT scholarships, and higher education tuition support.

Upper Secondary Education

High school education is non-compulsory. Admission requires passing entrance exams (Kōkō Juken). High school tuition support funds help offset costs for low-to-middle income households.
