The ¥650,000 Blue Return Deduction for Freelancers & Side Income Earners (青色申告)

Expected Outcome: Saved ¥65,000–¥200,000 in annual taxes
Required Documents Packet (Interactive Checklist)

Actionable Step-by-Step Procedure

1

Register as a Sole Proprietor (Kojin Jigyo-nushi) at the Tax Office

Submit an "Kaigyou Todoke" (Business Commencement Notification) to your local Tax Office (Zeimusho) — this is free and takes 10 minutes. You can also do this online via e-Tax.

Pro-Tip: File within 2 months of starting your freelance or side-income activity. If you miss this window, your Blue Return application defaults to the following tax year.
2

Apply for Blue Return (Ao Shinkoku) Status

Submit the "Shotoku-zei no Ao-iro Shinkoku Shonin Shinsei-sho" form at the same time as your Kaigyou Todoke or within March 15th of the target tax year. This unlocks the ¥650,000 deduction if you use double-entry bookkeeping.

Pro-Tip: Even using simple bookkeeping (without double-entry) grants a ¥100,000 Blue Return deduction — still better than the standard White Return (Shiro Shinkoku) with zero special deduction.
3

Use Cloud Accounting Software to Stay Compliant

Apps like Freee (freee.co.jp) or MoneyForward Me automatically categorize income and expenses, generate compliant double-entry books, and export tax return forms directly to e-Tax. Most plans cost ¥1,500–¥3,000/month.

Pro-Tip: Your software subscription cost itself is 100% deductible as a business expense under your Blue Return filing!

Did You Know?

If you earn even ¥200,000+ per year from any side income (tutoring, YouTube, Mercari shops, freelance design), you are legally required to file a tax return. The Blue Return system makes this legally advantageous, not just compliant.
Blue Return filers can carry forward net operating losses for up to 3 years — meaning a bad freelance year can reduce taxes in future profitable years.
