Opening Your First Japanese Bank Account on Day 1

🎉 Outcome: Saved 2 weeks of waiting
📋 Required Documents Packet:
  • Original Passport with valid visa
  • Residence Card with registered address on back
  • Japanese mobile phone number or company number
  • Personal Hanko/Inkan seal (or signature depending on branch)

Actionable Step-by-Step Procedure

1

Register City Hall Address First

Yucho Bank requires your address printed on the back of your Zairyu card.

💡 Pro-Tip: Go to City Hall in the morning, then visit Yucho Bank in the afternoon.
2

Use the Yucho Direct Web Application in English

Pre-fill your application online or via the mobile app to skip branch language barriers.

💡 Pro-Tip: Print out the barcode page to hand directly to the teller.
