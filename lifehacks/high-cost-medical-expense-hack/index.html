Capping Medical Bills with the High-Cost Medical Expense Benefit (Kogaku Iryohi)

Outcome: Saved ¥300,000+ on emergency medical treatment
Required Documents Packet:
  • National Health Insurance Card (Kokumin Kenko Hoken) or Shakai Hoken Card
  • Limit Application Certificate (Gendo-gaku Teyotyo Shomeisho)
  • Residence Card

Actionable Step-by-Step Procedure

1

Request Limit Application Certificate (限度額適用認定証)

Apply for this certificate at your local Ward Office (for NHI) or company Health Insurance Association BEFORE planned hospital admissions or costly procedures.

Pro-Tip: Presenting this card at hospital check-in caps your payment at your monthly ceiling upfront, preventing huge initial out-of-pocket payments.
2

Understand Your Income Bracket Cap

For standard earners (annual income ¥2.1M - ¥6.0M), out-of-pocket monthly medical expenses are capped at roughly ¥80,100 plus 1% of costs exceeding ¥267,000.

Pro-Tip: If you have 3+ qualifying high-cost months within a 12-month period, the cap drops further to ¥44,400/month (Tasukai-ketsugo).
3

Claim Self-Medication Tax Deduction (セルフメディケーション税制)

If annual OTC pharmacy drug purchases exceed ¥12,000, keep your receipts to deduct the excess amount on your annual tax return (Kakutei Shinkoku).

Pro-Tip: Look for the "Self-Medication Tax System" mark on OTC boxes of pain relievers, cold medicine, and allergy pills.
