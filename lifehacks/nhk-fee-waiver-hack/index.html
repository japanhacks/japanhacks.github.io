Legally Reduce or Waive Your NHK Subscription Fee (受信料免除)

Outcome: Saved ¥7,920–¥15,840/year
Required Documents Packet:
  • NHK Subscription Contract number or recent NHK invoice
  • Welfare Recipient Certificate (生活保護受給証明書) if applicable
  • Residence Card and Juminhyo

Actionable Step-by-Step Procedure

1

Check If You Qualify for a Waiver

Full (100%) waiver applies to: welfare recipients, residents of facilities for the disabled or elderly, households with a disability certificate holder (Techo), and single-person households with zero taxable income. 50% waivers apply to a wider range of low-income or student situations.

Pro-Tip: Even if you're not on welfare, if your annual income is below the inhabitant tax non-taxable threshold (approx. ¥1,000,000–¥1,350,000 depending on household size), you may qualify.
2

Obtain a Waiver Eligibility Certificate from City Hall

Request a "Jumin-zei Hikazei Shomeisho" (Non-taxable Income Certificate) from your ward office's tax desk. This document is the key proof NHK requires.

Pro-Tip: The certificate is free of charge. Bring it directly to your nearest NHK district office or submit via the NHK website.
3

Submit the Waiver Application to NHK

Contact NHK (0570-077-077) or visit nhk.or.jp to submit the discount application form along with your eligibility certificate. Approval is typically processed within 4–6 weeks and backdated to your application month.

Pro-Tip: Once approved, the waiver auto-renews annually as long as you continue to qualify. NHK will send a renewal confirmation slip each year.

Did You Know?

NHK is legally required to grant full waivers to qualifying households — they cannot refuse. The fee is ¥1,320/month (terrestrial) or ¥2,530/month (satellite) if you have a TV or device that can receive NHK.
Technically, owning any device capable of receiving NHK broadcasts (including some laptop screens or car navigation systems) creates a legal obligation to pay unless you have a waiver.
