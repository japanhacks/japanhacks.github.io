Furnishing Your Apartment for Under ¥30,000 via Hard-Off, Mercari & Sayonara Sales

Outcome: Saved ¥150,000+ on initial home furnishing
Required Documents Packet:
  • Residence Card
  • Japanese phone number (for Jimoty / Mercari account SMS verification)
  • Cash or credit card for transport / delivery fees

Actionable Step-by-Step Procedure

1

Use Jimoty (ジモティー) for Free Local Pickup

Jimoty is Japan's local classifieds app where departing residents post refrigerators, washing machines, microwaves, and tables for 0 JPY or minimal cost.

Pro-Tip: Filter by "あげます・譲ります (Giving away for free)" and message sellers who live within your ward for direct pickup.
2

Shop Suburban Hard-Off, Off-House & Treasure Factory Outlets

Visit secondhand mega-stores located on city outskirts for clean, tested home appliances backed by 6-month store warranties.

Pro-Tip: Stores usually offer cheap truck rental (軽トラ貸出) for 1–2 hours free of charge if you buy major appliances there.
3

Leverage Expat Sayonara Sales in Peak Months

Check Facebook groups ("Tokyo Freecycle", "Osaka Sayonara Sales") in March/April and August/September when departing expats sell entire room sets.

Pro-Tip: Offer to pick up multiple items at once to negotiate package deal discounts.
