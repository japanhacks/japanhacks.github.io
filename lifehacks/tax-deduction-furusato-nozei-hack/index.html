Hacking Resident Tax with Furusato Nozei (Hometown Tax)

🎉 Outcome: Get ¥50,000+ in gourmet food gifts

📋 Required Documents Packet:

  • G源泉徴収票 (Gensen Choshuhyo - Annual Income Statement)
  • My Number Card for One-Stop Special System

Actionable Step-by-Step Procedure

1

Calculate Your Annual Furusato Nozei Limit

Use an online simulator based on your taxable annual income and family structure.

💡 Pro-Tip: Only ¥2,000 out-of-pocket fee is retained; all remaining donations reduce next year's Resident Tax.
2

Select Gifts on Rakuten Furusato Nozei or Satofull

Choose municipal gifts like fresh rice, fruits, or hotel vouchers.

💡 Pro-Tip: Use Rakuten during Shopping Marathon event days for 10-20% back in points.
3

File One-Stop Special System (ワンストップ特例制度)

If donating to 5 or fewer municipalities, submit the simple 1-page form or scan via My Number app.

💡 Pro-Tip: No full final tax return required if One-Stop form is mailed before January 10th.
