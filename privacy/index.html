Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This policy covers the JapanHacks web platform and the JapanHacks mobile app. JapanHacks is an independent, privately run project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Japanese government or the Ministry of Justice (MOJ).

What we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we (via Supabase, our authentication provider) store your email address and, if you sign in with a password instead of Google, a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and basic profile info with us; we do not receive your Google password.
  • Community content. Questions, answers, and profile details you post in the community forum are visible to other users and are stored so the forum can function.
  • Payment information. Premium subscription payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your card number — Stripe handles that directly and shares with us only what's needed to manage your subscription (e.g. plan status).
  • Device-local data. Bookmarks, checklist progress, and calculator inputs are stored on your own device (browser local storage or mobile app state) and are not transmitted to us unless you're signed in and choose to sync them.

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the mobile app.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and keep you signed in.
  • To operate features you use directly: bookmarks, checklist tracking, the community forum, and premium subscriptions.
  • To respond to support requests and enforce our community guidelines.

We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run JapanHacks: Supabase (authentication and database hosting) and Stripe (payment processing). Each handles data under its own privacy policy and only for the purpose of providing that service to us. Community forum posts are visible to other JapanHacks users by design.

Your rights

You can update your profile information at any time from My Account. You can also delete your account and associated stored data at any time directly in-app using the "Delete Account" button in your account settings (My Account on web or the Account menu on mobile), or by emailing us at privacy@japanhacks.app from your registered address. Community posts you've made may be retained in an anonymized form to keep forum threads coherent for other users, unless you ask us to remove the content entirely.

Children's privacy

JapanHacks is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, notify signed-in users in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email privacy@japanhacks.app.
