Japanese Real Estate Negotiation Hacks

  • 💡 You can legally ask real estate agents to remove optional fees like Disinfection (消毒代 ¥16,500) and 24h Support (安心サポート ¥16,500).
  • 💡 Outside peak season (May–January), negotiate for 0 Reikin (礼金0) or 0.5 month Agency Fee (仲介手数料半額).
  • 💡 Move between May and December to save up to 40% on moving company rates compared to March/April.
