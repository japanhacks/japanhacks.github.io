Steps to Recover Unpaid Overtime Wages

  • ⚠️ Unpaid Overtime Alert: Under Article 37 of Japan Labor Standards Act (労働基準法第37条), your employer owes you approx. ¥47,500/month in unpaid statutory overtime allowances.
  • Under Japanese labor law revision, claims for unpaid wages can be claimed retroactively up to 3 years.
  • You can submit timecard records to your local Labor Standards Inspection Office (労働基準監督署 - Rōki) for free enforcement assistance.
