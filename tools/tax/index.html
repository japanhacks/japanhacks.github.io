Year 1 vs Year 2 "Resident Tax Shock" Visualizer

New residents in Japan pay ¥0 Resident Tax in Year 1 because Resident Tax (住民税) is calculated retroactively on your previous calendar year income. In June of Year 2, your monthly paycheck will decrease by ~¥19,525/month.

Furusato Nozei (ふるさと納税) Tax Credit Limit

By donating to Japanese municipalities, you receive regional gifts (wagyu beef, rice, travel vouchers) while deducting the donation minus ¥2,000 from your next year's Resident Tax bill.

Max Tax-Deductible Donation:
¥59,476
Out-of-pocket cost: ¥2,000
